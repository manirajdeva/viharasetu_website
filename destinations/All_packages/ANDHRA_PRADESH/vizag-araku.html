Vizag & Araku Valley

Visakhapatnam's beach promenades and hilltop viewpoints, then a winding drive through the Eastern Ghats to the coffee-scented hills of Araku Valley — the Bay of Bengal and the Borra Caves in one circuit. Best travelled September–February, when the coast is cool and Araku's hills stay green.

Signature Experiences

  • Sunset over the Bay of Bengal from Kailasagiri's hilltop park
  • The scenic Vizag–Araku toy train, threading tunnels and valleys past Borra Caves
  • A cup of Araku's own organic coffee, grown by the valley's tribal farmers
