North Goa, South Goa & Dudhsagar

The classic North-South Goa circuit extended inland — beaches and forts up north, Portuguese-era churches and quiet coves down south, and a jungle detour to Dudhsagar, one of India's tallest waterfalls. Best travelled October through March, when the monsoon has cleared and the coast and falls are both at their best.

Signature Experiences

  • Fort Aguada at sunset and the beach shacks of Baga & Calangute
  • Old Goa's UNESCO-listed churches — Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral
  • A jeep safari through the Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary to Dudhsagar Falls
