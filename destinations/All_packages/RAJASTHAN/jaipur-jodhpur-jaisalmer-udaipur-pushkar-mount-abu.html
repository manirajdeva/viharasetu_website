Jaipur, Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Udaipur, Pushkar & Mount Abu

The complete Rajasthan circuit — the Pink City's palaces, the Blue City's fort, the golden dunes of the Thar Desert, the lakes of Udaipur, the sacred waters of Pushkar, and the hill-station calm of Mount Abu. Best travelled October through March, when the desert heat has eased and every fort and lake is at its most inviting.

Signature Experiences

  • Amber Fort and Hawa Mahal in Jaipur, the Pink City
  • Camel safari and a desert camp under the stars near Jaisalmer's golden dunes
  • Sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola in Udaipur and the sacred ghats of Pushkar Lake
