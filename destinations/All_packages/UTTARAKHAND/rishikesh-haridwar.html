Rishikesh & Haridwar

Rishikesh & Haridwar — twin holy towns on the Ganges, where the river leaves the Himalayas and enters the plains. We arrange Ganga Aarti evenings, yoga sessions, and heritage walks across both riverside towns.

Signature Experiences

  • Evening Ganga Aarti at Har Ki Pauri and Triveni Ghat
  • Yoga and meditation sessions with local teachers in Rishikesh
  • Heritage walk through Haridwar's temples and bazaars
