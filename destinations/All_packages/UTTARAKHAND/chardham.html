Chardham

Chardham — the sacred Himalayan circuit of Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath. We arrange comfortable pilgrimage stays, local guides, and a well-paced itinerary through Uttarakhand's holiest shrines.

Signature Experiences

  • Guided darshan at all four Himalayan shrines — Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath & Badrinath
  • Trek and pony/palanquin support on the Kedarnath and Yamunotri routes
  • Riverside stays along the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi

Do Dham Yatra — 7 Nights: day-by-day plan

Kedarnath and Badrinath, the two most-visited shrines of the circuit.

  1. Day 1: Arrival & Drive to Guptkashi. Arrive, drive towards Guptkashi for acclimatisation. Includes: Dinner
  2. Day 2: Guptkashi to Kedarnath Base. Drive to Sonprayag, trek to Kedarnath base. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  3. Day 3: Kedarnath Darshan. Trek/pony ride to Kedarnath Temple. Darshan and overnight near the shrine. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  4. Day 4: Return to Guptkashi. Trek back down, drive to Guptkashi. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  5. Day 5: Guptkashi to Joshimath. Drive to Joshimath via Rudraprayag. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  6. Day 6: Joshimath to Badrinath. Drive to Badrinath. Evening darshan at the temple. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  7. Day 7: Badrinath Darshan. Morning darshan, Mana village visit. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  8. Day 8: Departure. Drive back and depart. Includes: Breakfast
Chardham Express — 9 Nights: day-by-day plan

All four shrines on a comfortable, well-paced pilgrimage route.

  1. Day 1: Arrival & Drive to Barkot. Arrive, drive towards Barkot for Yamunotri. Includes: Dinner
  2. Day 2: Yamunotri Darshan. Trek to Yamunotri Temple, darshan and return. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  3. Day 3: Barkot to Uttarkashi. Drive to Uttarkashi towards Gangotri. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  4. Day 4: Gangotri Darshan. Drive to Gangotri, temple darshan. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  5. Day 5: Uttarkashi to Guptkashi. Drive towards Guptkashi for Kedarnath. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  6. Day 6: Kedarnath Darshan. Trek/pony ride to Kedarnath, darshan. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  7. Day 7: Return & Onward. Trek back down, drive towards Joshimath. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  8. Day 8: Badrinath Darshan. Drive to Badrinath, temple darshan. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  9. Day 9: Mana Village. Mana village and the source of the Saraswati river. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  10. Day 10: Departure. Drive back and depart. Includes: Breakfast
Complete Chardham Yatra — 12 Nights: day-by-day plan

An unhurried circuit of all four dhams with extra acclimatisation days.

  1. Day 1: Arrival & Acclimatisation. Arrive in Haridwar/Dehradun, rest and acclimatise. Includes: Dinner
  2. Day 2: Drive to Barkot. Drive towards Barkot for Yamunotri. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  3. Day 3: Yamunotri Darshan. Trek to Yamunotri Temple, darshan. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  4. Day 4: Barkot to Uttarkashi. Drive to Uttarkashi. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  5. Day 5: Gangotri Darshan. Drive to Gangotri, temple darshan. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  6. Day 6: Uttarkashi to Guptkashi. Drive towards Guptkashi. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  7. Day 7: Rest Day. Acclimatisation rest day in Guptkashi. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  8. Day 8: Kedarnath Darshan. Trek/pony ride to Kedarnath, darshan, overnight. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  9. Day 9: Return from Kedarnath. Trek back down, drive towards Joshimath. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  10. Day 10: Badrinath Darshan. Drive to Badrinath, temple darshan. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  11. Day 11: Mana Village. Mana village and Vasudhara Falls viewpoint. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  12. Day 12: Leisure Day. Rest day before the return journey. Includes: Breakfast, Dinner
  13. Day 13: Departure. Drive back and depart. Includes: Breakfast
