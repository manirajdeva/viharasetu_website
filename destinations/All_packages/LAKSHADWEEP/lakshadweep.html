Lakshadweep

Lakshadweep — India's smallest union territory, a scatter of coral atolls and turquoise lagoons in the Arabian Sea. We curate island-hopping stays, reef diving, and lagoon experiences across Agatti, Bangaram, Kavaratti, and Minicoy. Best travelled October through May, when the seas are calm and the reefs are at their clearest.

Signature Experiences

  • Scuba diving and snorkeling over pristine coral reefs
  • Glass-bottom boat rides across turquoise lagoons
  • Sandbank picnics on the uninhabited islet of Bangaram
